- Same 16:9 presentation canvas and Together palette

- No fake UI, charts, numbers, words, or logos
- Investor evidence and diagrams remain native HTML/SVG, not AI-generated art
- The system must support both emotional public storytelling and restrained
  private analytical pages

## Directions

1. **Documentary Square** — candid, editorial, human, and emotionally credible.
2. **Architectural Square** — tactile spatial models that make place and density

3. **Graphic Square** — a distinctive screen-printed human constellation that

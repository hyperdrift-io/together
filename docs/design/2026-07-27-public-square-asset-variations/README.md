# Public Square asset variations

**Status:** Awaiting asset selection

## Approved idea

The Public Square remains the visual metaphor: many independent people already
share one suitable public place; two people recognise that their interest is
mutual; one warm opening makes a face-to-face hello possible now.

These variations refine the asset only. They do not change the approved
proposal/investor information architecture, homepage, copy, product mechanics,
or investor evidence visualisations.

## Shared art direction

The eye enters on a single warm human circle inside a deep midnight field.
Exactly two independent people become the focal point without looking posed,
paired, isolated, tracked, or exposed. The place must feel public and socially
alive. The image should communicate proximity and mutual possibility in 50ms.

All three assets are:

- text-free and logo-free
- generated with the workspace Recraft pipeline
- Recraft V4, `1344x768` (`16:9`)
- normalised from Recraft WebP output to true PNG
- composed to tolerate responsive cropping
- free of hearts, location pins, maps, radar interfaces, phones, tables,
  dating-profile cards, London landmarks, and fabricated data

## Variations

### 01 — The Human Halo

A cinematic aerial photograph of a lived-in public gathering. The surrounding
movement stays natural while a warm architectural pool of light reveals two
independent people near its centre.

**Strength:** most immediate and human.

**Trade-off:** realism can make the moment feel event-specific.

### 02 — The Living Square

A tactile, isometric architectural miniature of one public place at night.
Buildings, paths, and people form a coherent social ecosystem around one warm
central courtyard.

**Strength:** strongest continuity with the approved investor-board asset.

**Trade-off:** less emotionally intimate than photographic people.

### 03 — The Mutual Constellation

A bold editorial screen-print seen from directly above. Independent human marks
move through one public field; two complete figures and one shared amber opening
form the unmistakable centre.

**Strength:** most ownable, iconic, and adaptable across subpages.

**Trade-off:** supporting copy must carry more of the literal human story.

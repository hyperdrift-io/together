# Together subpage asset systems

**Status:** Awaiting visual-direction approval

## Scope

This gate covers the asset language for:

- the public proposal
- the private investor memo
- the private registration admin
- confirmation, inbox, removal, and privacy surfaces

It does not redesign the approved Passing Glance homepage or change subpage
information architecture, copy, data, access control, or product scope.

## Shared design brief

The eye enters on one unmistakable human signal against midnight navy. The
assets should make two independent people sharing one real place feel possible,
mutual, and immediate without depicting a staged date or fabricating product
mechanics. The emotional tone is warm precision: editorial rather than stock,
premium rather than decorative, and human without sentimentality. Proposal
imagery carries desire; investor visuals carry strategic coherence; admin and
transactional surfaces use quieter fragments of the same language. Verified
investor figures and charts remain native, deterministic visualisations rather
than generated imagery.

## Constants across all three boards

- Brand palette: midnight `#061521`, near-black `#020a10`, paper `#fffaf0`,
  gold `#f4b544`, vermilion `#ef4a24`, muted mineral blue
- Existing Together wordmark and editorial typography
- Same representative page set and responsive scope
- No hearts, dating-app cards, swiping, chat bubbles, location pins, London
  clichés, staged couples, fake charts, glassmorphism, or generic gradients
- One dominant asset per public surface; minimal derived motifs elsewhere

## Generation

- Generator: workspace `scripts/generate-recraft-asset.mjs`
- Model: Recraft V4
- Canvas: `1280x832` (`3:2`, the supported V4 landscape size)
- Recraft raster output was normalised from WebP bytes to real PNG files with
  `sips` after generation.
- Generated copy is indicative only. Production copy remains the reviewed,
  native page copy.
- These are approval boards, not production assets. Production crops and
  responsive variants will be generated only for the selected system.

## Directions

### 01 — Human Signals

Documentary editorial photography keeps two independent adults inside a lived
social atmosphere. A small warm alignment—not a beam, pin, or UI effect—makes
mutual recognition legible.

**Strength:** the fastest emotional identification and closest continuity with
Passing Glance.

**Trade-off:** casting and photographic quality must stay exceptional or it
will collapse into conventional dating advertising.

### 02 — Luminous Cartography

Architectural public-space geometry becomes an ownable visual language:
topographic layers, two warm signal points, and a shared aperture in a field of
independent movement.

**Strength:** the most coherent system across customer emotion, investor
density, admin operations, and quiet transactional states.

**Trade-off:** it is less immediately intimate than showing recognisable
people.

### 03 — Shared Aperture

Two complete sculptural forms create a third luminous opening between them.
The opening—not either person—is the signature, expressing privacy, mutuality,
and new possibility without prescribing a venue.

**Strength:** the most premium, minimal, and distinctive symbolic language.

**Trade-off:** supporting copy must preserve the literal same-place,
face-to-face proposition.

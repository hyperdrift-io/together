Create a visually iconic, spectacular 16:9 homepage hero image for Together, a service that helps two adults who are already at the same real public social place discover mutual interest and say hello face to face.

Metaphor: THE OPEN CIRCLE. A contemporary public square during a vibrant social evening, viewed almost directly from above. Architecture, paving, pools of warm light and many believable adults moving and gathering form a vast imperfect circular composition. The circle is deliberately open at one point: an inviting human-scale threshold rather than a broken or sad symbol. On the two sides of that opening, two relatable adults remain within separate small social groups yet share a subtle moment of recognition across only a few real metres. Their positions complete the visual rhythm without turning them into a posed couple. The metaphor should read instantly: life already contains the connection; Together reveals the opening and people decide whether to step through it.

Visual direction: premium editorial aerial photography fused with restrained graphic composition, geometrically powerful and highly ownable. Deep midnight navy ground, warm amber pools of social light, a restrained coral accent, soft ivory highlights. A dense, diverse crowd around the outer rhythm, believable gestures and groupings, tactile stone and architectural detail, dramatic contrast, elegant film grain. The incomplete circle must arise from the real square and crowd choreography, not from a drawn icon, halo, magic portal or interface overlay.

Responsive composition: keep the open-circle story in the central 50 percent; leave calmer dark texture in the upper-left for homepage copy; preserve the threshold on narrow crops.

Avoid: central isolated couple, lonely figures, ring or wedding symbolism, broken-heart imagery, touching or kissing, staged romance, empty date table, spotlight, hearts, phones, app screens, swipe cards, chat bubbles, location pins, maps, radar, surveillance, London landmarks, literal glowing connection line, fake text, letters, numbers, logos, charts, generic gradients, glassmorphism. No typography.

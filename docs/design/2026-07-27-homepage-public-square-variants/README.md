# Together homepage — Public Square challengers

Status: awaiting visual selection. Do not implement.

These three images challenge the current Passing Glance hero without changing
the product promise: Together helps two adults already sharing a public social
place discover mutual interest and turn it into a comfortable face-to-face
hello.

## Comparison constants

- Same 16:9 desktop hero canvas
- Same deep navy, warm amber, coral, and ivory palette
- No words, logos, UI, phones, hearts, pins, maps, or dating-app tropes
- No isolated or staged central couple
- Two adults remain embedded in independent social contexts
- The visual metaphor expresses nearby possibility, mutual recognition, and
  human agency; the product reveals an opening rather than manufacturing love

## Directions

1. **The Human Current** — a spectacular public-square crowd whose movement
   opens a subtle path between two independent social groups.
2. **The Shared Horizon** — an eye-level social gathering where a band of warm
   light makes a nearby possibility suddenly legible.
3. **The Third Light** — two ordinary pools of social light overlap to reveal a
   brighter shared possibility that did not exist in either space alone.

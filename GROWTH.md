# Together — Growth

## Mission

Turn one availability choice into a mutually accepted, fully arranged
face-to-face date in London.

## Current experiment

Test whether qualified London adults register for the app after seeing its
competition-relative promise:

> A meeting. Not a match.

The landing page is a demand test, not the product.

## Variant

`aida_arranged_date`

Hypothesis: a positive, concrete picture of an already-arranged date plus an
email-only commitment will produce credible demand without the friction of
pretending product onboarding already exists.

## Primary path

`landing_viewed → launch_interest_started → launch_interest_submitted → launch_interest_confirmed`

Primary metric:

`launch_interest_submitted / landing_viewed`

Guardrail:

`launch_interest_confirmed / launch_interest_submitted`

## Build threshold

Do not begin product development until the experiment reaches at least 500
qualified London visits, 100 confirmed registrations, and 15% confirmed
conversion across at least three independent sources.

Rework at 8–14.9%. Stop or change the wedge below 8%.

## Privacy boundary

Analytics may contain city, source, campaign, device, variant, and a
non-sensitive registration identifier. It must never contain the email address
or later dating-profile answers.

## Launch gate

Do not recruit public traffic until durable intake, confirmation, consent and
privacy copy, GA4, and PostHog are implemented and verified.

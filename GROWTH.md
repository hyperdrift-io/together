# Together — Growth

## Mission

Get Londoners out of matching-and-messaging loops and into a confirmed
face-to-face meeting with someone who has chosen to be there.

## Current experiment

Test whether qualified London adults respond to the outcome contract:

> A meeting. Not a match.

The first public behaviour is `meeting_request_submitted`, not a generic email
signup.

## Primary path

`landing_viewed → meeting_request_started → meeting_request_submitted`

The full product path is:

`meeting_request_submitted → meeting_followup_accepted → introduction_accepted → meeting_confirmed → meeting_attended → meeting_would_repeat`

## Privacy boundary

Growth analytics may contain city, source, cohort, form step, and structured
timing or meeting-format categories. They must never contain names, contact
details, free text, detailed dating preferences, access needs, or safety
reports.

## Launch gate

Do not recruit public traffic until durable intake, privacy and conduct terms,
the responsible operator, the guarantee remedy, GA4, and PostHog are confirmed
and verified.
